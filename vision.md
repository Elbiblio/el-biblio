Elbiblio MVP Summary
Core Purpose

A spiritually-centered social growth app that helps people grow through:

tribe-based belonging,
time-bound spiritual commitments,
lightweight shared reflections,
and daily spiritual growth prompts.

The MVP should focus on:

consistency, belonging, and emotional support

—not content overload, AI novelty, or too many disconnected features.

Core MVP Flow
1. User Onboarding
Sign up
Choose visibility mode:
Anonymous
Initials
Nickname
Public profile
2. Spiritual Compass

Users complete a short spiritual alignment assessment to:

understand themselves,
identify growth tendencies,
and discover compatible tribes.
3. Tribe Selection

Users join one or more spiritually aligned tribes based on:

mindset,
life season,
or growth goals.

Tribes are identity/community layers, not denominations.

4. Commitment Challenges (Core Engagement Engine)

Users join:

private or public
time-bound spiritual commitments/challenges.

Examples:

30-day social media fast
+5 min daily prayer for 1 month
30 days gratitude challenge
40 days discipline challenge
40 days daily scripture reflection
50 days forgiveness journey

Users can choose from getting 3 - 10 nudges (push notifications) per day based on their type of committment. The nudges also serve as a way to check in/complete the challenges daily.

5. Challenge Reflection Feed

The feeds feature becomes available once you join a commitment challenge

members post short reflections (max 500 chars) - maximum of once a day,
share struggles/progress,
react/support others.

ONLY members inside the same commitment challenge see your reflections in their feed.

This is the app’s primary social mechanic.

6. Weekly Rituals

Simple recurring moments:

Weekly reflection hub - special hub that you can only post on during weekends (saturdays/sundays), all reflections are auto deleted after one month but you can bookmark them. Scoped only to your tribe

Audio hangouts - create or join one (set who can join and max no - tribe only, challenge only, or everyone)


7. Daily Faith Growth Questions

Users optionally answer short daily spiritual/philosophical questions.

Each answer includes:

concise explanations,
spiritual insight,
practical perspective,
and difficult real-world context.

Examples:

suffering,
temptation,
discipline,
forgiveness,
doubt,
purpose.
AI Usage (Minimal)

AI should only support:

encouragement,
summaries,
nudges,
recap generation,
and reflection assistance.



Features to Deprioritize or Remove From MVP
Too many AI personalities
Complex gamification
Excessive analytics
Large public social feeds
Deep theological content systems
Overly detailed assessments
Too many tribe layers
Feature-heavy journaling
Broad community discovery systems

UI/UX Revamp
Must adopt consistent icon usage for every core achievement/completion based on the core vision of the app (i.e. you complete identity assessment, join a committment/challenge, you join a tribe, you invite another user, you complete your daily committment/challenge etc. Every step that feels like a milestone in your usage of the app or personal growth
