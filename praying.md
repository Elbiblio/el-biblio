The ‘How to pray’ module should work like a comprehension and questions module that lets the user read the entire content as laid out below then answer key questions showing they understand the steps required to start living a true and prayerful life as citizens of the Kingdom.

The Forgiveness module should work like a meditation module (long/short form) starting first with a 3-10 minutes ‘Reflection’ to think about their actions today and how they could have done it better. Then Prayer for forgiveness’, (3-5 minutes) based on Pslams or selected Bible passages talking about Forgiveness as well as some of Pauls passages talking about forgiveness (let them choose directly or by typing a vice to pray for while starting the module), and finally 2-5 minutes penitence (thanksgiving for the grace to pray/heal/reconcile, pledge to work better with grace and do better next time)

The Holy Spirit module should basically remind the user that God has already finished the work of the Kingdom by bringing us the Holy Spirit, and the great prophets of the past hoped upon for that day where ‘God with us’. The Holy Spirit is Gods own sanctifying grace and power directly in us. But we have to learn its nature to work better with it.
It does not force itself on us, it has to be invoked/desired (woe if you want, David did).
It reveals all of Gods plan for us on a need basis. It is our sacred icon of trusting and learning to trust God. It has the power to do everything yet its primary role in us is to save us and therefore we must recognize that:
we have no need to fear the world with the Holy Spirit
we see children of the Kingdom not ‘strangers’ with the Holy Spirit
we now live out a full Kingdom citizenship with the Holy Spirit, our marker, our ID card that goes as far as speaking the right word for us when we cannot.
the Holy Spirit supplies all our needs - as far as it is line with God’s plan for us 

How to Pray

As God’s children to be and even now we already are for He made us in His image, the world could be compared to a nursery for us. But in the way of true love and perfection, good virtues ironically have to go through patient perseverance and trust (which can be often misunderstood as evil/bad) to be forged. The troubles and rough play of the world are only a part of the journey to perfection where souls are forged and tested. We should not mistrust, dislike or hate our Father and Master because of the forging process because it is indeed the perfect and best way. Sin like dirty tattered clothes/bleeding wounds may dishonor/discredit us from approaching God but God is never far from us, immediately we have a true intent to approach and dress in fine linen and heal our wounds, he’s every ready to do it Himself. He’s not offended by the wound himself directly but being perfect love, his children hurting/getting wounded in the forging process displeases him and he draws even closer to those that need such tending. The wound is only there because being perfect Love, he also gave us freewill and though only Him can absolutely choose perfect good all the time, by starting us with so little knowledge and putting himself in us, there is hope we will learn to appreciate that the fastest way to complete the forging process is to let his Spirit in us grow, and for many they take a very long time to trust and say yes to the full autonomy of His spirit. Yet even when we say yes, His spirit of love does not impose or take over our affairs but illuminates us with divine knowledge and shows us the best ways according to the divine plan.

Jesus came to ‘fast-forward’ the grand divine plan not only by opening the doors of heaven and creating the greatest hospital of all time for our wounds/spiritual diseases, but also unlocking our citizenship, such a miracle! Before Jesus, it was so hard to practice our sonship/citizenship as God’s children or children of the Kingdom. Now by believing in Jesus and praying through His name, every single day, people are bringing the Kingdom even more forward. Yet even this great power lies so much hidden in a tangible world where tangible power like money and fame/authority can strike fear, bend wills and brainwash many. These tangible powers exist because the physical world is as of now cursed and therefore there is perceived scarcity and strife which don’t truly exist but exist now through population growth and division of labour. As such, these powers will certainly fade once the new Kingdom is established because the things you can buy with money in the perfect Kingdom are zero, there’s no scarcity, there is no strife. Love for yourself and for the Kingdom and Obedience is what truly makes you do things in the real Kingdom. Ambition is also worthy but dangerous and so we must be careful not to exceed the boundaries of our design because the one who made us is Perfect.

In order to pray to God therefore, we must recognize the overwhelming work He has put in to make us and to birth a new Kingdom and grace us with so much a status as His own children even though the cost for him is pain. Like a mother in Labour, the Almighty will surely see the entirety of the fruits of His work. The only question we have to ask ourselves is have we embraced His grand design and plan for us all for everything we need is now revealed to us.

Jesus taught us that we must pray with a Kingdom mindset. Everything now is about the Kingdom.

‘Our Father’ not ‘My Father, not my Family' s father. But ‘Our Father’. He is ‘the Father’ of all and his current most desire is the birth of His Kingdom. When therefore we pray, we pray to greet and adore His sovereignty, and to show our commitment towards His plan while we also ask him who is the perfect Father and provider for our daily needs. And we must also ensure that we bring no grudges before him so that there will be no case against us. God is the perfect father and provider, bearing a grudge almost suggests He is not doing his work properly and is also not an attitude of grace towards our neighbour and if our grudge be of perfect justice, can we face perfect justice? By recognizing and trusting that He has surrounded us with His help in form of angels and knows of our every situation, we will have no reason for fear or alarm and even though we fear, as long as we talk to Him with reverence and even the littlest of understanding, He is ever ready to teach us and correct us and bless us.

Learning to pray is not about learning magic words you can say to your dad (though learning things like please, sorry helps), but it’s more about recognizing who your dad is. The moment you truly do, talking to him becomes second nature because you realize how much you need him. God gave us our conscience as a basic compass, but it’s not enough to unlock the full talents he has endowed us with. This is often why we need Him most and he has now given us the Holy Spirit for this purpose. To put it a bit of an unorthodox way, bearing fruits 100-fold is more important than hiding or running from sin. Bad thoughts or evil desires are almost like consequences of our fallen nature and turning to choose ourselves briefly over God foolishly, yet His power is already greater than Sin, and our core and essential design is towards the establishment of the Kingdom to which we have been accorded with talents and a spiritual nature. Knowing this nature requires walking with God and it's like the spiritual equivalent of career discovery and knowing our true work as citizens of the Kingdom. Praying as a daily habit is also a journey towards learning the full extent of these talents we have been given and accounting for this work daily towards the establishment of the Kingdom.

Praying for forgiveness is critical for we are all unholy before God. Even the Cherubims and Seraphim fall down at his sight because he dwells in irreproachable light. The least we can do therefore everytime we pray is to approach with reverence and pray for forgiveness/cleansing in addition to our word offerings and petitions.
