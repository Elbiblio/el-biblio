When we grow spiritually/consume and digest the word of God, what we essentially obtain are virtues. # Fear of God
Fear most times sounds like a negative energy

How then is the fear of God also termed the beginning of Wisdom…?

The reason is simply because it’s truly not a negative energy in the sense it’s used but actually a wise energy.

As children of God we are only allowed to fear He who preserves our lives and through whom we exist. Any other fear is unholy and does not glorify God unless such fear is an indirect reverence to God, e.g. reverence of a fellow man who has certain authority from God over your affairs. Despite this, reverence is not the same as fear and you absolutely have nothing to fear from anyone if you’ve done nothing wrong. People often cite Elijah who ran but in his case, he caused God’s judgment on evildoers and their blood was enough to cause harm to him though of course not without God’s permission. Jesus also withdrew from the Pharisees when they schemed to kill him. It was not fear that made him avoid and hide but rather fear of God, not wanting to test God by going to where his crucifixion fate could be fast-forwarded.

Fear of God can be simply termed as a primordial spiritual sense of self-preservation and reverence to God and the spiritual order of things. The same way a child learns that a fire burns and can kill, a child will spiritually learn that sin ‘burns’ and can kill. We are warned that the enemy prowls about like a roaring Lion (1 Peter 5:8). And God’s first response to Cain the moment he became indignant was a warning that Sin was crouching at his door (Genesis 4:7). 

Every time we sin, we soil our clothes making ourselves nearly unpresentable. Continuous sin without fear of God could be likened to one who goes to the house of the drug addict, first they pay with money, then they run out of cash and give their belongings, then they give their energy, then they have nothing left and become completely enslaved with their lives almost entirely in the hand of the drug seller.

Fear of such enslavement by drug dealers is therefore not a negative energy but a wise energy that helps you avoid the temptation of choosing drugs as a ‘fix’ to any situation.

Furthermore God is Almighty and Holy and gives us a most great privilege by offering to dwell in us. Toying with that privilege could be likened to being cured of drug addiction and then installed as the president of a country/steward answering directly and only to God himself with a golden orb worn on your heart and a crown on your head, both of which begin to turn black whenever you take drugs.

Imagine the shame when you would go knocking with your crown and adornment at the house of the drug dealer after you have been cured.

As such, the fear of pain (aka. Self preservation) sometimes is sheer wisdom and has nothing to do with negative energy. The fear of falling down from the edge of a wall is not a denial of God's ability to save us from falling, but simply a recognition of the truth of the damage such a fall would cause if it were to happen.
